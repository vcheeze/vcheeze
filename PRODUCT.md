# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors evaluating the person behind the **vcheeze** alias — hiring managers, collaborators, and peers — who need a clear sense of who this is, what they’ve made, and whether to reach out. The site owner maintains and updates the presence.

## Product Purpose

A personal/portfolio site: the public home for work, writing, and contact under the vcheeze identity. Success is a visitor understanding the person and next step (hire, collaborate, follow) without friction.

## Positioning

Public identity under the alias **vcheeze** (GitHub handle), used the way creators use a distinctive handle as brand — not a product SaaS, not a generic “developer portfolio template” voice.

## Operating Context

Personal site on the web. Content, projects, and proof come from the owner. Stack already in repo: SvelteKit, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn-svelte, mdsvex. Current UI is still the default SvelteKit scaffold.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Intended surface: personal/portfolio presence (home, work/proof, contact, and related pages as built).
- No production portfolio content or routes beyond the scaffold yet.
- Undecided: exact page set, content inventory, domain/deploy target, and which projects or writing ship first.

## Brand Commitments

- Public name/alias: **vcheeze**.
- Binding visual constraints (user-specified; keep simple): background `#F1EDE4`, accent `#0E4D4E`, foreground `#292F29`.

## Evidence on Hand

No portfolio copy, project case studies, testimonials, press, or personal photography in the repo yet. Future work must not invent clients, roles, metrics, or quotes.

## Product Principles

1. **Alias first** — vcheeze is the identity signal; the site should feel like that person’s place, not a template.
2. **Simple by commitment** — respect the declared three-color, low-complexity look; do not layer ornamental systems on top.
3. **Proof over claims** — show real work and words when they exist; never fabricate evidence.
4. **Visitor clarity** — one clear path to understand who this is and how to connect.
5. **Owner-maintained** — structure and content should stay easy to update as the portfolio grows.
